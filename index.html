How to upload new code to your Cubetto+:

• Make sure the Cubetto robot is turned OFF

• Turn ON the control board, then hold the blue button until the board starts blinking

• Click Connect and select PRIMO INTERFACE in the popup window

1. Click Modify on any block to edit its behaviour.

2. Add steps and define the sequence.

3. Use Simulator to preview the movement.

4. Click Upload new settings to send to Cubetto.

5. Or upload default settings anytime.
